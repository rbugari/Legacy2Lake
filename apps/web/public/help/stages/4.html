Stage 4

Governance

Governance turns technical output into auditable evidence. It captures findings, produces report-oriented outputs, and prepares the project for final handoff.

Start here

Use Overview or Report to understand where governance stands and what the result means.

Main action

Run Governance to produce the report, findings, and certification outcome.

Why it matters

This is where the solution becomes understandable and defensible for delivery.

What to review

  • Report for certification signals and findings
  • Logs while Governance is running
  • Remaining issues that should be understood before Handover

Common mistake

Treating Governance as a ceremonial pass instead of the place where findings should be understood.

Before you continue

Make sure the report is available, the findings are understood, and the team is comfortable with the final delivery evidence.
