Stage 1

Triage

Triage converts inventory into decisions. It is where the platform decides what matters, what can be ignored, what needs review, and what is ready for downstream generation.

Start here

Use Overview first, then move into Grid and Graph as the main workbench views.

Main action

Run Analysis to populate the mesh, classifications, and the main structural interpretation.

Why it matters

Good Drafting depends on good Triage. Weak classification produces weak outputs later.

What to review

  • Grid for classifications and operating decisions
  • Graph for relationships and structural sanity checks
  • Schema and Mapping before approving Drafting
  • PII and project context when business rules matter

Common mistake

Treating Triage as a purely visual step and not reviewing the classification decisions before generation.

Before you continue

Confirm the important assets, sensitive data, and obvious ignores are all in a sensible state.
